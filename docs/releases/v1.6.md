## zanzibarr v1.6 — send it to the TV, and obfuscated releases just work

Stream anything from Usenet. Own nothing. Wait for nothing. zanzibarr plays NZB content **without downloading it** — instant start, frame-accurate seek, and nothing left behind on your disk.

### What's new in v1.6

- **Cast to your TV.** A new cast button in the player discovers Chromecast and AirPlay devices on your network and hands the stream over — play, pause, seek and volume mirror from the app, local playback pauses while you cast, and your position is kept when you disconnect, so continue-watching stays exactly right. The receiver fetches straight from zanzibarr's local engine over a token-gated LAN endpoint; nothing ever leaves your network. (Chromecast verified; AirPlay video is best-effort pending real-hardware tests.)
- **Obfuscated NZB sets just work.** Scene releases that rename every RAR volume to random hashes — stripping extensions and scrambling volume order — used to fail with "split chain flags are corrupt". The engine now sniffs archive signatures from content, reads each volume's embedded RAR volume number, and reassembles the chain in the correct order, including posters that skip numbering the first volume.
- **Subtitle font picker.** Pick between bundled Noto Sans, Serif and Mono faces from the subtitle controls; your choice is remembered per title alongside the existing color picker. (Applies to text subtitles — ASS keeps its authored styling.)
- **Android: NZB downloads fixed.** Opening an NZB straight from indexer search results no longer trips over scoped-storage restrictions — downloads now land in the app's own cache directory.
- **macOS: minimum system version is now 12.0** (was 10.15), aligning with the current media stack.

### Already inside

- **Play instantly, never download** — the Rust engine fetches only the segments you actually watch
- **Built-in indexer search** — Newznab search with quality badges, tap-to-play inside the app
- **True seek** — yEnc `begin/end` segment mapping, accurate even on 100+ GB releases
- **Archive-aware** — multi-volume RAR5/RAR4 (incl. `-hp` encrypted) and 7z (STORE, LZMA/LZMA2 solid, AES-256) reassembled in memory
- **Compressed RAR, seekable** — volumes that actually compress stream too: decode-ahead serves the player while unpacking continues
- **PAR2 repair** — missing/corrupt segments rebuilt with Reed-Solomon, byte-exact against par2cmdline
- **Full picture pipeline** — SDR / HDR / HDR10 / Dolby Vision selector with BT.2390 tone mapping; TrueHD/Atmos, DTS-HD MA, AV1
- **Continue watching** — playback positions remembered per title, with a resume row on the home screen
- **Smart Canvas** — draw your own crop with live aspect preview, applied on the GPU
- **In-app updates** — zanzibarr checks for new versions itself; Android installs them in place with one tap
- **Player essentials** — PiP, frame stepping, speed, audio sync, external audio/subtitle loading, double-click ±10 s seek, subtitle color picker, fit ↔ fill toggle
- **14 languages** — EN, TR, ES, DE, FR, PT, IT, RU, ZH, JA, KO, HI, AR, FA (RTL included), dark & light themes
- **Private by design** — NNTP credentials live only in the OS keychain; no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud

### Getting started

1. Install the build for your platform.
2. Open the settings gear and enter your Usenet provider's NNTP details.
3. Open any `.nzb` (or search with the built-in indexer) and press play.
