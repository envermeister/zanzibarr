## zanzibarr v1.7 — flawless Android TV remote navigation

Stream anything from Usenet. Own nothing. Wait for nothing. zanzibarr plays NZB content **without downloading it** — instant start, frame-accurate seek, and nothing left behind on your disk.

### What's new in v1.7

- **Android TV, fully remote-driven.** Settings, indexer setup and search are now completely navigable with a D-pad — no mouse, no workarounds. Up/down always moves between controls; left/right leaves a text field only at the edge of the text (caret movement inside text is preserved); the password/API-key eye buttons no longer steal focus from their fields — reach them with right-arrow at the end of the text, and left-arrow brings you back; the indexer's Test/Save buttons are stacked so both are reachable; and the focused control finally shows a visible accent outline everywhere. Settings opens with focus on the first control (no on-screen keyboard surprise), and when search results arrive, focus jumps straight to the first card — press select, watch. (GitHub issue #4 — thanks @MrMookdog for the video report.)
- **Regression-proof TV navigation.** The whole remote chain is covered by new end-to-end tests that inject real D-pad key events — from the settings form down to both save buttons, and from the search field to playing the first result — so TV navigation can't silently break again.

### Already inside

- **Play instantly, never download** — the Rust engine fetches only the segments you actually watch
- **Cast to your TV** — Chromecast & AirPlay; the receiver pulls straight from zanzibarr's token-gated LAN endpoint
- **Built-in indexer search** — Newznab search with quality badges, tap-to-play inside the app
- **Obfuscated NZB sets** — hash-named RAR volumes identified by content, reordered by embedded volume numbers
- **True seek** — yEnc `begin/end` segment mapping, accurate even on 100+ GB releases
- **Archive-aware** — multi-volume RAR5/RAR4 (incl. `-hp` encrypted) and 7z (STORE, LZMA/LZMA2 solid, AES-256) reassembled in memory
- **Compressed RAR, seekable** — volumes that actually compress stream too: decode-ahead serves the player while unpacking continues
- **PAR2 repair** — missing/corrupt segments rebuilt with Reed-Solomon, byte-exact against par2cmdline
- **Full picture pipeline** — SDR / HDR / HDR10 / Dolby Vision selector with BT.2390 tone mapping; TrueHD/Atmos, DTS-HD MA, AV1
- **Continue watching** — playback positions remembered per title, with a resume row on the home screen
- **Smart Canvas** — draw your own crop with live aspect preview, applied on the GPU
- **In-app updates** — zanzibarr checks for new versions itself; Android installs them in place with one tap
- **Player essentials** — PiP, frame stepping, speed, audio sync, external audio/subtitle loading, double-click ±10 s seek, subtitle color & font pickers, fit ↔ fill toggle
- **14 languages** — EN, TR, ES, DE, FR, PT, IT, RU, ZH, JA, KO, HI, AR, FA (RTL included), dark & light themes
- **Private by design** — NNTP credentials live only in the OS keychain; no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud

### Getting started

1. Install the build for your platform.
2. Open the settings gear and enter your Usenet provider's NNTP details.
3. Open any `.nzb` (or search with the built-in indexer) and press play.
